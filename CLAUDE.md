# CoatSim — notes for development sessions

- Goal: polished, usable thin-film coating simulator for catheters and IOL cartridges (owner's SAICCS machine: plasma station → coating → horizontal airflow "HACS" → cure).
- Layout: `src/core/*` = physics (pure ES modules, no DOM). `src/ui/*` = interface. `build.mjs` bundles everything into `docs/index.html` (GitHub Pages) and `dist/coatsim.html`. Never hand-edit `docs/index.html`.
- Bundler constraints: single-line `import {…} from` statements; `export function|const|class` only; exported names must be unique across all modules.
- Always run before committing: `node build.mjs && node test/core.test.mjs && node test/process.test.mjs`. For UI changes, also run `node tools/ui-test.mjs <presetId> /tmp/shots` (look at the screenshots), `node tools/ui-controls-test.mjs`, `node tools/ui-sweep-test.mjs /tmp/shots` and `node tools/ui-compare-test.mjs saiccs_ha_heated /tmp/shots` (A/B comparison). For graphics work, `node tools/ui-look.mjs <presetId> /tmp/look` captures the 3D view alone (oblique and side-on at the tip, at fill / plug / tip pool / end) and the render time; `COATSIM_HTML=<old build>` renders an older build for before/after.
- SAICCS facts from the owner: cartridges are stationary (no rotation) and vertical, wide proximal end up and tip down (tilt 0 is the default for every stage on an `iol_cartridge`; see `defaultTilt`). Inserter nozzles dispense coating into the wide proximal end, driven by a peristaltic pump; during the initial insertion, "vacuum" peristaltic pumps on the distal tips withdraw the coating, then stop while filling continues, so the coating dwells ~30 s in the full cartridge (held by positive pressure in the withdraw line). After the dwell the withdraw pump restarts and the cartridge is essentially fully drained before the heated airflow/cure (confirmed by the owner). Modelled by the `fill` stage. In HACS, heated air blows down into the cartridge at 20–60 SCFH and 60–80 °C (default 30 SCFH at 60 °C) to cure the inside, and horizontal nozzles blow across the tip opening into the long side of the bevel, angled to match the bevel, so excess is ejected straight off the tip without disturbing the coating on the exposed bevel face. Cartridge tips are typically a 35° bevel, measured between the bevel face and the axis; `tipBevelDeg` uses that convention. Nothing rotates: there is no rpm option anywhere (the owner asked for this; `withDefaults` forces rpm 0), and spray coverage comes from fixed nozzles around the part. Thermal-cure coatings (HA) otherwise cure stationary in an oven.
- `node tools/run-presets.mjs [id]` gives a headless regression of all presets (each should finish in < 60 s at the normal grid).
- Keep `docs/ROADMAP.md` current: tick items when done, add findings.
- Bump `version` in package.json for user-visible changes.
- Commit messages should explain the physics or UX reason for each change.
