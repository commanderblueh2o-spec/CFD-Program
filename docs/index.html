CoatSim — how it works

Quick start: pick a preset, change settings on the left (tick Live to re-run automatically), press ▶ Run, and watch the coating flow, dry and cure in the Animation tab. Colours: glossy blue = wet liquid (darker = thicker), sand = dried film, amber = cured, grey = bare.

CoatSim solves the thin-film (lubrication) equations on the coated surface of a catheter, tube, IOL cartridge or flat coupon, the standard model for coating flows (drainage, levelling, wetting, sag and beading).

  • Wetting: contact angle from surface energies of the part and coating, with plasma activation and hydrophobic recovery.
  • Gravity and capillarity: drainage for any orientation; beading on thin wires and collars in bores.
  • Air: heated down-draft through the bore (air cools along the bore; the wall heats up with a lag), horizontal blow-off jets, vacuum air draw.
  • Film formation: solvent evaporation, solids build-up, thermal (oven or hot air) or UV crosslinking up to gelation.
  • Application: dip, spray, needle/drip insertion (several beads possible), vacuum withdraw.

Comparing runs. After a run, press 📌 Set as reference A in the results panel, change a setting and run again: the panel compares the two runs and lists what changed, and Show difference map colours the part by how much thicker (orange) or thinner (blue) the final dry film is than in A. The reference is kept until you clear it, also across presets and page reloads.

Limits. Contact lines are resolved down to the grid size, puddles comparable to the bore size are approximate, and the material values are typical. Calibrate viscosity, solids and drying rate against one measured film before relying on absolute thickness. The tool is best for comparing options and seeing trends.

Controls. 3D: drag to rotate, right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Ctrl+Enter runs, Space plays/pauses. Click a stage in the timeline to jump to it.
